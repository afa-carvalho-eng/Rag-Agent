# Expenses and travel

This is a fictional handbook written as sample data for the RAG agent.

Business travel is booked through the travel desk. Flights under four hours are booked in economy class. Hotel stays are reimbursed up to 160 euros per night, or 220 euros in capital cities.

Meals during travel are covered by a daily allowance of 45 euros, so individual meal receipts are not required. Any other expense needs a receipt and must be submitted in the expense tool within 30 days of the purchase.

Expenses above 500 euros need written approval from the department head before the purchase is made. Approved expenses are reimbursed with the next monthly payroll run.

Each employee has a yearly training budget of 1,500 euros for courses, conferences and books, plus five paid training days.
