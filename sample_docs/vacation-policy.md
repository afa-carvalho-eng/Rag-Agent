# Vacation policy

This is a fictional handbook written as sample data for the RAG agent.

Every full-time employee at Northwind Robotics receives 25 days of paid vacation per calendar year, in addition to public holidays. Part-time employees receive a share proportional to their weekly hours.

Vacation requests go through the HR portal and need approval from the direct manager. Requests for more than five consecutive working days must be submitted at least four weeks in advance.

Up to five unused vacation days can be carried over into the next year. Carried-over days expire on 31 March and are not paid out.

During the first three months of employment, vacation is limited to the days already accrued, at a rate of roughly two days per month.
